The Solution

YSenseAI: Consent-First Data Platform

🛡️

Z-Protocol v2.1

Tiered consent recorded with the document version, and an honest definition of withdrawal: exclusion from future training, not erasure from already-trained models

🔐

Tamper-Evident Attribution

SHA-256 fingerprint, author identifier and verification on every contribution. Key-based signatures and C2PA manifests are next

💰

Fair Compensation

15-30% of licensing revenue by content tier, written into the terms before any deal exists. No deal exists yet, and we say so

🌍

Cultural Protection

Community attribution and benefit sharing for traditional knowledge

October 2026

Where We Are, Honestly

Z-Protocol v2.1 asks us to tell the truth about limitations. This is the current state, not the roadmap.

✅

What runs today

An open-source beta you run on your own machine: story canvas, five-layer extraction, consent records, library, export. One maintainer.

⚠️

The demo is a mock-up

The Hugging Face Wisdom Canvas shows the flow but does not call an AI model. Its layer text and scores are placeholders.

⚠️

Attribution is tamper-evident, not signed

Fingerprints prove integrity. Key-based signatures that prove authorship, and C2PA manifests, are the next engineering step.

⚠️

No money has changed hands

No AI lab has licensed anything yet. The revenue share is a term in the agreement, not a payment history.

Full detail: Known Limitations in the README and the October 2026 audit and market report.
